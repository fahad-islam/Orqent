# Orqent-owned canonical IDs

Orqent will mint its own canonical IDs for Customer Accounts, Customer People, Deals, Contracts, Workflow Runs, Proposed Actions, and related coordination records. External systems remain the operational source of truth for their own records, but Orqent uses canonical IDs and Identity Links to coordinate work across providers and to avoid coupling the domain model to any one CRM, support, or contracts provider.
